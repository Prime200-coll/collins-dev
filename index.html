About me

An engineer first, a web developer by choice.

I'm Collins, a computer engineer who builds websites. Engineering trained me to think in systems: how the pieces connect, where things break and how to make them reliable. I bring that same precision to every website I build.

Today I build company websites, portfolios, landing pages and online stores for businesses and individuals. Each project starts with a simple conversation: what do you sell, who needs to find you, and what should they do when they arrive? Then I design, build and launch a site that looks sharp on any phone and loads quickly.

I care about the details people feel but rarely name: the spacing that makes a page easy to read, the load time that keeps a visitor from leaving, the button that sits exactly where a thumb expects it.

I have built Next.js storefronts for phones and cybersecurity products, so I understand what it takes to turn visitors into customers.
